# Club vaults — whitelist-gated share transfers, a beneficial-owner cap, and affirmative-approval voting (SHE-197, SHE-201)

## Why

**A "private" vault whose shares can be handed to anyone is not private.** The vault's depositor whitelist gates exactly one thing: who may be the `receiver` of a mint. `_requireApprovedDepositor` (`src/SyndicateVault.sol:2039-2041` on main @ `a5399c7`) is called from precisely two sites — `_deposit` (`:2202`) and `requestDeposit` (`:2335`) — and from nowhere else. The share token itself is an unmodified `ERC20Upgradeable`/`ERC20VotesUpgradeable`: `SyndicateVault` overrides no `transfer` or `transferFrom`, and its single `_update` override (`:1445-1491`) does two unrelated jobs (high-water-mark reset at `:1471-1473`, receiver auto-delegation at `:1488-1490`) and **imposes no transfer restriction of any kind**. A whitelisted depositor can therefore mint into a closed vault and, in the next transaction, transfer those shares to any address on earth. Nothing anywhere counts how many addresses hold shares.

That gap is the whole of the compliance posture. Vault shares are securities-shaped: an investor pays money into a common enterprise and expects profits from an agent's efforts. The two paths Sherwood is building toward both depend on the share register staying inside a boundary the contract enforces. **Reg D 506(c)** permits general solicitation only if every purchaser is verified accredited — the SEC's July 2026 CDI blesses onchain attestations as reasonable-steps verification, but a verification regime that a secondary transfer walks straight out of verifies nothing. **ICA §3(c)(1)** exempts a fund held by fewer than 100 beneficial owners with no public offering — an exemption the protocol cannot claim while the number of beneficial owners is unbounded and unobserved. The whitelist is the register; today it leaks on transfer, and the count is not kept (SHE-197).

**Pass-by-default voting is a weak fact in the same argument.** The governor's optimistic model (`ProposalLifecycle._computeState`, `src/ProposalLifecycle.sol:77-110`) approves a proposal at `voteEnd` unless AGAINST votes reach `vetoThresholdBps` of live supply; silence passes. That is a deliberate and good default for an agent-run fund, but under Howey it reads as *profits from the efforts of others* with the members' role reduced to a veto they must actively exercise. A club posture — a vote before every buy, and no buy without one — is a materially different fact pattern, and it is the posture members of an investment club actually want (SHE-201). It needs an affirmative-approval mode, not a replacement of the optimistic one.

**Owner decision (2026-08-31): receiver-side gating only, and no new register.** The transfer gate checks the RECEIVER against the existing `_approvedDepositors` set and never the sender. This matches the deposit gate's documented asymmetry — "compliance attaches to the share holder", `src/SyndicateVault.sol:577-582` — and it is also, at zero extra storage, the grandfathering rule for a vault that flips from open to whitelist mode: an existing holder outside the list may always send OUT and redeem, and simply cannot receive more. The alternative (a separate `transferAllowed` register, or a sender-side check with a grandfather set) was rejected: it adds a second membership list that can drift from the first, and a sender-side check would trap holders in a fund they never agreed to be locked into. Full rationale in design.md D2/D4.

## What Changes

- **`SyndicateVault._update` becomes the gate.** It is already "the single OZ chokepoint" for every mint, burn and transfer (its own comment, `src/SyndicateVault.sol:1466-1468`). While the vault is in whitelist mode (`_openDeposits == false`, `:173`), a share movement whose `to` is not in `_approvedDepositors` SHALL revert a new named error `ReceiverNotApproved`. The exemption set is exact and each member is load-bearing: `to == address(0)` (burn) and `from == address(0)` (mint — already gated at the deposit sites, and re-gating it here would break nothing but duplicate a check); `from`/`to == address(this)`; **both directions of the withdrawal-queue escrow**; and `value == 0`, which preserves the permissionless delegation heal documented at `:1484-1487`. No event for a rejected transfer — a revert with a named error is the whole signal.
- **A `maxHolders` cap on distinct nonzero-balance addresses.** New vault parameter, `0 = uncapped`. Counted in the same `_update` path: increment on a zero→nonzero receiver, decrement on a nonzero→zero sender, with the withdrawal queue and the vault itself excluded from the count (neither is a beneficial owner). The cap is checked only on a movement that would CREATE a new holder. `maxHolders != 0` SHALL imply whitelist mode, and the two flags SHALL be mutually constrained at their setters — an open-deposit vault with a holder cap is trivially griefable by dusting fresh addresses until the cap fills (design.md D7).
- **A per-vault `approvalMode`.** `Optimistic` (default, unchanged) or `Affirmative`. In `Affirmative` mode `_computeState`'s `Pending` branch (`src/ProposalLifecycle.sol:77-110`) tests `votesFor >= liveSupply * approvalQuorumBps / 10_000` instead of the veto inequality; failing it resolves `Rejected` with `reviewConcluded == false`, exactly as a veto rejection does today (`:102-105`), so no registry economic commit fires and the existing dead-proposal review-cancel path (`:272-280`) still runs. Absence of votes is rejection. Guardian review, execution, settlement and every downstream state are untouched.
- **`approvalMode` / `approvalQuorumBps` join `GovernorParameters`** under the existing vault-owner-instant + `whenNoActiveProposal` discipline (`src/GovernorParameters.sol:194-292`), with hardcoded bounds validated in `_validateParamBounds` (`:163-192`) so the factory rescue path cannot seat what a setter would reject. `approvalQuorumBps` bounded **[2_000, 6_700]** — matching the shape of `MIN_VETO_THRESHOLD_BPS`/`MAX_VETO_THRESHOLD_BPS` (`:43-44`) with a ceiling low enough that a single large holder's absence cannot make passage impossible. Both snapshot onto the proposal at Draft→Pending beside the existing `vetoThresholdBps` snapshot (`src/SyndicateGovernor.sol:1362`, `:1610`), as APPENDED `StrategyProposal` members.
- **The club "template" is a documented parameter bundle, not contract code.** Once features 1–3 land, a club vault is: `openDeposits = false`, `maxHolders = 99`, `approvalMode = Affirmative`, a default `approvalQuorumBps`, and `agentFeeBps = 0` — the last already per-vault owner-settable with an explicit-zero encoding built for exactly this (`src/SyndicateVault.sol:1427-1433`, storage `:201-207`). The only protocol surface the bundle needs is that `maxHolders` and the two governor params be seatable at creation: `SyndicateConfig` (`src/SyndicateFactory.sol:79-86`) and `ISyndicateVault.InitParams` (`src/interfaces/ISyndicateVault.sol:127-136`) each gain fields; the named preset lives in the CLI and docs. **No template contract, no preset enum, no registry.** (design.md D10.)
- **Storage goldens regenerated, append-only**: `script/syndicate-vault-layout.golden.json` (new vault fields carved from the front of `__gap`, `src/SyndicateVault.sol:525`) and `script/syndicate-governor-layout.golden.json` (two appended `StrategyProposal` members), each with an explicit review-the-diff task. Both contracts are gated by `script/check-layout-goldens.sh:271-297` and by `test/VaultLayoutPins.t.sol` / `test/governor/GovernorLayoutPins.t.sol`.

## Non-goals

Stated explicitly so review does not have to infer them:

- **No onchain KYC, accreditation, or geo logic.** The vault learns nothing about who an address is. The whitelist stays an owner-curated address set; whatever verification produced it happens off-chain and is out of scope here.
- **No VPN, IP, or jurisdiction detection anywhere**, in contracts or in tooling.
- **No migration of live vaults.** Every parameter this change adds defaults to today's behavior (`maxHolders = 0`, `approvalMode = Optimistic`), so an existing vault is byte-for-byte unchanged until its owner opts in.
- **No change to fee mechanics.** `agentFeeBps` and the management fee are referenced only as parameters the club bundle sets; neither their arithmetic, their bounds, nor the waterfall moves.
- **No change to the guardian review gate**, its economic commit, coverage, tiering, or the withdrawal queue's own accounting.
- **No secondary-market venue, no transfer-approval workflow, no forced transfer / clawback.** A rejected transfer reverts; there is no admin override that moves someone else's shares.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. Both affected capabilities already exist under `openspec/specs/`.

### Modified Capabilities

- `syndicate-vault`: "Vote checkpointing and auto-delegation" gains the transfer guard and holder accounting that share its `_update` hook; "Depositor access control" extends from a deposit gate to a share-holding gate and acquires the `maxHolders` cap and its mutual constraint with `openDeposits`; "Async redemption requests (Lane B)" and "Request cancellation" gain the queue-escrow exemptions that keep the Lane B round trip open under the gate; two new requirements pin the transfer gate and the beneficial-owner cap.
- `syndicate-governor`: "Optimistic passage with veto threshold" becomes mode-selected rather than universal; "Governance parameter management" gains `approvalMode` / `approvalQuorumBps` with their bounds; "Factory creation of syndicates" seats the club parameters atomically; two new requirements pin affirmative passage and the club creation preset.

## Sequencing (hard ordering — record of 2026-08-31)

**Features 1 and 2 are one vault-side unit and land together; feature 3 is independent; feature 4 lands last because it is only wiring over 1–3.**

1. **Vault-side (features 1 + 2) before anything else.** They share one `_update` body and one golden regeneration; splitting them means regenerating `script/syndicate-vault-layout.golden.json` twice and reviewing two append-only diffs for one logical change.
2. **Governor-side (feature 3) is orderable either way, but its `StrategyProposal` append collides with two in-flight changes.** `per-call-capital-declarations` and `proportional-quorum-sizing` both append to the same struct and both regenerate `script/syndicate-governor-layout.golden.json`. Whichever lands last rebases and regenerates; the append-only rule makes the merge mechanical but the golden diff MUST be re-reviewed, not re-accepted. tasks.md §0 carries the re-verification.
3. **Feature 4 last.** It is a factory + interface change over parameters that must already exist.
4. **Naming collision to avoid:** `proportional-quorum-sizing` uses "quorum" for guardian *coverage* (`requireApproveQuorum`, `quorumTierThreshold`). This change's `approvalQuorumBps` is an LP *vote* quorum. They are unrelated mechanisms; the spec text and any new natspec MUST say which quorum it means at every mention.
